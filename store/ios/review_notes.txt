App Review notes — ADL ShareFlow (iOS) Build 83 (1.0.9)

What this app is:
  A group expense splitter for friends, family, roommates, and trips.
  It is not a bank, wallet, money transmitter, or payment processor.
  Friends settle outside the app (Bit / PayBox / bank). The app only shows who owes whom.

What this app is not:
  There is no Pro plan, no subscription, and no coming-soon paid feature.

Demo login:
  Email + password: see repo file `.store_review_account.local`
  (generated by: cd backend && python scripts/setup_app_review_demo.py)

Demo group on that account:
  «קבוצת הדגמה — App Review» with sample expenses.

Account deletion:
  Profile → Delete account (two confirmations).
  Personal data is removed immediately. Shared group expenses may remain for other members.

Other notes:
- Hebrew RTL UI by default; English is available in Profile → language.
- Deep links: https://adlshareflow-production.up.railway.app/join/{code}
- Push notifications require the system permission prompt.
- Camera is used to photograph a receipt attachment or scan a QR join code.
- Users may photograph or attach a receipt image to an expense. The image is stored for reference. ShareFlow does not automatically scan or extract receipt data.
